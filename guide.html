How to use Postmatch

Postmatch keeps your match history and stats. After a game, you import a screenshot of the end-of-match screen, check the numbers, and save. It is free, it works without a connection, and your matches never leave your phone.

1. Add it to your Home Screen

Do this first. Matches saved in a browser tab and matches saved in the Home Screen app are kept apart, so pick one and stay with it.

iPhone, in Chrome: tap the share button in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

iPhone, in Safari: tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android, in Chrome: tap the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Open Postmatch from the new icon from now on.

2. Set your in-game name

Open Settings and type your in-game name exactly as it shows in matches. Postmatch uses it to find your row, and needs it when the screenshot shows the items view.

3. Log a match

  1. When the end-of-match screen appears, take a screenshot. On iPhone, press the side button and volume up together. On most Android phones, press power and volume down together.
  2. Open Postmatch and tap Import screenshot. On Android you can also share the screenshot to Postmatch from your gallery.
  3. Choose the screenshot. Postmatch reads it in a few seconds, on your phone.
  4. Check the numbers against the strip from your screenshot at the top. Anything marked “Check this” was hard to read. Pick your hero, then tap Save match.

If Postmatch asks “Which row is yours?”, tap your row. If a value is wrong, tap it and type the right one. Nothing saves until you tap Save.

Import the original screenshot. A screenshot sent through a chat app is shrunk and is too small to read. Pick it from your Photos or gallery instead.

No screenshot? Tap Enter a match by hand.

4. See your stats

Matches shows your win rate and average kills, deaths and assists. Choose a hero at the top to see only those matches. Stats shows your record with each hero. Tap any match to change or delete it.

5. Back up your matches

Your matches are stored only on this phone. If you remove the Postmatch icon, lose the phone, or clear your browser data, they are gone unless you have a backup.

  1. Open Settings and tap Save a backup file.
  2. Save the file somewhere off the phone: Files and iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

Postmatch reminds you after 10 new matches. To move to a new phone, install Postmatch there, open Settings, tap Restore from a backup file and choose the file. Restoring replaces any matches already on that phone.
